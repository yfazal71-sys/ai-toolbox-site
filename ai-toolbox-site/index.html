Why AI Toolbox?

AI Toolbox is a set of free online tools for writing, images, PDFs and data. Each tool does one job on its own page, loads fast on a phone, and has plain instructions. Students can count words and shorten notes, creators can compress images and convert formats, and developers can check JSON with the code formatter.

Privacy comes first

Your text or file stays in your browser and is not uploaded to our server. Tools run with JavaScript on your device, and we do not ask for an account or personal details. Read the privacy policy for details.

Frequently asked questions

Are the tools really free?

Yes. There is no sign-up and no paywall.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Every tool on AI Toolbox runs in your browser, so your text and images stay on your device.

Are these tools powered by AI?

Some are helpers such as the summarizer and prompt generator that use simple algorithms and templates in your browser. Tools that need a large AI model, like translation and background removal, are planned and will say clearly how they process your data.

Who can use AI Toolbox?

Students, writers, creators and developers. It works on phones, tablets and computers.
