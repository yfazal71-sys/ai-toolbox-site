What is the Image Compressor?

An image compressor re-encodes a picture with lower quality settings so the file is smaller. Smaller images load faster and are easier to email or upload.

How to use it

  1. Choose an image
  2. Pick JPG or WebP and adjust quality
  3. Download the compressed file

Example

A 3 MB phone photo at quality 75 often drops below 1 MB with little visible change.

Benefits

Faster pages, faster uploads and less storage used.

Frequently asked questions

Is my image uploaded?

No. Compression runs in your browser.

Why did my PNG get bigger or lose transparency?

JPG has no transparency, and PNG is lossless. Use WebP to keep transparency.
