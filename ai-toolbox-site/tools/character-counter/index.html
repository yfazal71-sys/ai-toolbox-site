Character Counter

Count characters with and without spaces, letters, digits and lines. Free, no sign-up.

Privacy: Your text or file stays in your browser and is not uploaded to our server.

What is the Character Counter?

A character counter shows the exact length of your text. Many platforms limit posts, meta descriptions or form fields by characters, not words.

How to use it

  1. Paste your text
  2. Check characters with and without spaces
  3. Trim the text until it fits your limit

Example

"Hi 2 you" has 8 characters with spaces and 6 without.

Benefits

Helps you stay inside limits for posts, titles and SMS messages.

Frequently asked questions

Are emoji counted as one character?

Most single emoji count as one. Some joined emoji count as several.

Are line breaks included?

Yes, a line break counts as one character in the with-spaces total.
