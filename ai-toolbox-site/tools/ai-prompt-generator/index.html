Prompt Generator

Build a clear, structured prompt for any AI chat assistant. Free, no sign-up.

Privacy: Your text or file stays in your browser and is not uploaded to our server.

What is the Prompt Generator?

A good prompt states the task, the audience, the tone and the format. This tool fills a proven template with your details, so you can paste the result into any AI assistant.

How to use it

  1. Describe your task
  2. Set the audience, tone and format
  3. Copy the prompt into your AI chat

Example

Task "Explain photosynthesis" for students in a friendly tone as bullet points produces a ready-to-paste prompt.

Benefits

Better prompts usually mean fewer follow-up messages.

Frequently asked questions

Does it call an AI model?

No. It builds the prompt text locally. You choose where to use it.

Can I edit the result?

Yes. Copy it and change anything you like.
